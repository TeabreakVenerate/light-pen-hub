# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Objective
Build an interactive community hub for "Author Light Pen" to monetize their work, engage with readers, and track analytics. The platform will serve as a central nexus that directs readers to external reading sites (like Webnovel) while keeping the community, commenting, and financial support in-house. A core goal is building systems that elevate engagement and accessibility, particularly targeting youth in Africa and a global fiction audience.

## The Author Personas
The platform must reflect the author's brand and three distinct pen names:
1. **Light Pen:** Creator of handsome, suffering male leads.
2. **Author Eliora:** Emotional romance weaver.
3. **Heavenly_Daoist:** Sudden raging creative madness.

## Core Features
1. **Persona-Driven UI:** The site visually shifts to match the vibe of the selected pen name.
2. **Book Portals:** Dedicated pages for each book featuring:
   - High-fidelity 2.5D interactive book covers ("Click for a surprise" mechanics).
   - Prominent outbound links ("Available on: Webnovel, etc.").
   - Real-time comment sections for community theorizing and discussion.
3. **Stripe Gifting Mechanism:** Frictionless micro-transactions for readers to support the author per-book.
4. **Live Viewer Ranking:** A real-time leaderboard updating instantly when a reader sends a gift.
5. **Admin Dashboard:** Tracks overall gift income, outbound link click-through rates, and page interactions.
